# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, engineering managers and frontend engineers evaluating Suman's work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that makes projects, career history, technical focus, resume and contact easy to assess.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Primarily static Next.js site with a dark-default Light/Dark toggle and a Server Action contact form. Preserve supplied content and assets. Never invent metrics, employment, availability, technologies or project outcomes. No analytics, WebGL, scroll-jacking or custom cursor. The owner explicitly requests a rotating star background inspired by the original live site, official employer logos and their current LinkedIn portrait. Accessibility, performance and factual accuracy precede decorative effects.

## Evidence on Hand

Original content in the session backup, existing portrait and project screenshots in public, supplied resume, GitHub and LinkedIn links. Stale career dates and inconsistent stack claims require owner review; see docs/CONTENT_REVIEW.md.

## Brand Commitments

Personal and restrained; dark-first with an equally considered light theme, clear typography and minimal motion. User explicitly authorized comprehensive redesign and continued implementation after audit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic links and headings, keyboard access, visible focus, labeled forms, reduced-motion behavior, responsive layouts down to 320px.
